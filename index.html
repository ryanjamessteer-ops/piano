Piano Instant

Notes fall toward your keys and wait for you. Play the right key on your real piano and the microphone catches it. No sheet music, no cables.

  1. Stay quiet for 3 seconds so the app can learn your room's hum and filter it out.
  2. Play every C, lowest to highest. Then run up all the white keys, then all the black keys. The app learns each key by its place in the run, however it sounds, and saves them, so you only do this once.
  3. Pick a song. Every note it asks for is a key the app already knows.
